# Hold the Light

*A story about caring for someone you love — and yourself.*

A browser game about supporting a loved one's mental health, made for **W4GZ (Writing for Gen Z)**.

Someone you love is going through a hard time. Walk through your home, find the lights, and choose how to respond in each moment.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any browser (phone or desktop). No install or internet connection needed, apart from loading fonts.

1. Choose your character (Man, Woman or Non-binary) and your loved one (Man, Woman or Non-binary). Names are optional.
2. Explore six rooms, opened chapter by chapter (Week 1 → Week 3 → Month 2). Within a chapter, visit rooms in any order.
   Each grey, faded object (a guitar, a bowl of soup, a phone…) is a moment with your loved one: pick the response you think helps most and it comes back to life. Finish a room to bring in a comfort item.
3. Keep an eye on:
   - 💗 **Trust**: grows with supportive answers and drops with hurtful ones. The Bedroom only opens at trust 60.
   - 🔋 **Energy**: every talk costs energy. Recharge in Your Corner, where some ways to rest work better than others.
   - 👀 **Reading the moment**: sometimes they need quiet company, sometimes your full attention.
   - 🌧️ **Setbacks**: as weeks pass, a light may go out on a bad day.
4. Reach one of three endings: *A Growing Spark*, *A Steady Light* or *A Safe Harbor*.

## A note on safety

This game is for learning. It is not a substitute for professional help. If someone may be in danger, contact your local emergency number. To find a free, confidential helpline in your country, visit [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com).
